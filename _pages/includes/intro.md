# 👨🏻‍💻 About Me

Hi! I am **Matt Zhang** (张锐), a master's student in Artificial Intelligence at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT), graduating in 2027. I am currently a research intern at **GigaAI**, working on embodied foundation models and VLA post-training.

<p style="color: #5a5a5a; font-size: 0.92em; margin: -0.25em 0 0.9em;">Hi！我是 <strong>Matt Zhang（张锐）</strong>，北京邮电大学人工智能硕士研究生，2027 年毕业。目前在 <strong>GigaAI（极佳视界）</strong> 做研究实习，方向是具身基础模型与 VLA 后训练。</p>

My research focuses on **post-training of vision-language-action (VLA) models**, especially reinforcement learning post-training for real-robot manipulation — action chunking, long-horizon task structure, and contact-rich assembly. More recently I have been exploring **agentic robotics**: embodied agents that plan, act, and verify their own behaviour, with the long-term goal of building robots that reliably handle multi-stage tasks in the real world.

<p style="color: #5a5a5a; font-size: 0.92em; margin: -0.25em 0 0.9em;">我的研究聚焦于<strong>视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型的后训练</strong>，尤其是面向真机操作的强化学习后训练——action chunking、长时序任务结构、富接触装配。最近我在探索 <strong>Agentic robotics</strong>：能够规划、执行并验证自身行为的具身智能体；长期目标是让机器人可靠地完成真实世界中的多阶段任务。</p>
