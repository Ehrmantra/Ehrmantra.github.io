# 👨🏻‍💻 About Me

Hi! I am **Matt Zhang** (张锐), a master's student in Artificial Intelligence at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT), graduating in 2027. I am currently a research intern at **GigaAI**, working on embodied foundation models and VLA post-training, and leading the home-scenario track of our WRC project.

My research focuses on **post-training of vision-language-action (VLA) models**, especially reinforcement learning post-training for real-robot manipulation — action chunking, long-horizon task structure, and contact-rich assembly. More recently I have been exploring **agentic robotics**: embodied agents that plan, act, and verify their own behaviour, with the long-term goal of building robots that reliably handle multi-stage tasks in the real world.
